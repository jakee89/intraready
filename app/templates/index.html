Field cheat sheet

Arrival date

The day goods reached Malta. This decides the reporting month.

Country of consignment

The EU country the shipment physically came from.

Country of origin

Where the goods were manufactured. It can differ from the supplier country.

Net mass

Weight of goods without packaging. Shipment gross weight is not a substitute.

Invoice value

Value of goods in EUR for the declaration, rounded only at export.

Statistical value

Goods plus relevant transport/insurance costs under the applicable rule.
